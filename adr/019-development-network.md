**Model (Burn event and address)** · `model:devnet:burn` A qualifying event is a settled on-chain destruction output of the declared test asset with a positive, explicit integer amount and an independently checkable unspendable script. The output commits one permanent target burn address in its signed transaction before broadcast; missing, ambiguous or wrong-run target encodings are refused. The address is the declared chain-run identity paired with an opaque permanent name selected for the consumer's authenticated binding; it is not the destruction outpoint and makes no assertion about a person or wallet owner. Each destruction outpoint identifies one evidence event and contributes at most once to its committed target. Multiple fresh destructions may increase that target's amount; no event or already recorded amount may be moved, merged or reassigned to another address. Do not infer attribution from a change address, wallet label or mutable receipt. destroyamount illustrates on-chain destruction, but its ordinary null-data output alone supplies no target binding and its wallet comment is not on-chain evidence. The profile's target-bearing constructor and decoder require the evidence in (`amend:devnet:burn-targets`). Fees, ordinary transfers, grants, other assets and unconfirmed outputs contribute no burn amount.

**Rule (Mathematical interface)** · `rule:devnet:burn-map` The test burn-amount map at an address is the exact sum of distinct qualifying pinned destructions represented in the recognized marker policy's accepted sets and committed to that address, zero when none have been accepted, with 100000000 units per displayed token. The opaque address space is defined by (`model:devnet:burn`). Declare a bootstrap capacity bound of 1000000000000 units, backed by a fixed initial supply of that many units, no subsequent issuance of that asset, no reachable reissuance authority and no peg or subsidy minting it; the faucet opens at a published boundary strictly after the run starts, following a declared nonempty pre-opening interval. That opening is the external-acquisition boundary for this test accounting. The entire initial test-asset supply is held for the faucet, the only source of newly distributed test asset; no operator distribution, fee payout of undistributed test asset or other route may bypass (`rule:devnet:faucet-limits`). Fee funding must not create such a route. Transfers of already distributed asset do not create supply. Declare a unit-cost projection of 1 test-asset unit irreversibly consumed per burn unit, at the profile's published terms when destruction occurs. This equality follows from choosing the same test-asset and burn unit and counting destruction as the sole proportional outlay, not from a universal lower bound. Publish any transaction or recording-event cost separately with its denomination and terms; never fold it into the amount-proportional projection. The published faucet schedule bounds release into circulation, not the later rate at which holders burn accumulated balances. These are the four test counterparts of ``def:comparator:burn-interface``; depth, chain provenance and operator-bound evidence remain disclosures, not extra mathematical inputs. A finite faucet quota is not proof of a finite supply or bootstrap bound.

**Caveat (Economic assurance)** · `cav:devnet:assurance` The fixed supply and sole-source faucet schedule provide a declared supply curve in test-asset units: recipient and settled-window quotas bound release, and every qualifying burn consumes a resource whose initial quantity and release rate are known and limited (`rule:devnet:burn-map`), (`rule:devnet:faucet-limits`). **Rule (Faucet budgets)** · `rule:devnet:faucet-limits` This is the published release schedule for the sole source of newly distributed test asset, drawn only from the fixed supply in (`rule:devnet:burn-map`), with no reissuance.
