# Peer Network implementation design · `spec:design:implementation`

## Purpose and scope · `sec:design:purpose`

**Setup (Authority)** · `setup:design:authority` The normative paper edition is network-v0.25.1. This design realizes (`spec:vision:concept`) through public contracts, algorithms and evidence obligations in the sense of (`spec:xchg:design`). Paper labels are exhibited, repository labels are cited, and data-dependent statements follow (`rec:render:decision`). The release and current answer status govern over an older provisional reading in (`reg:questions:overview`).

**Convention (Design boundary)** · `conv:design:scope` The contracts below constrain later domain bites; they do not assert that those crates or their evidence exist. Deployments provide public choices and boundary facts, while later bites choose reviewed dependency versions and concrete storage layouts within these contracts. Consensus, persistence operations, burn realization, guild policy, rewards, bridge, CAN and licence enforcement remain outside (`conv:vision:operations`) and (`conv:vision:terminal-systems`).

**Invariant (Public causes)** · `inv:design:public-causes` A binding operation consumes an immutable input object, an explicit effective declaration and its stated prior-boundary evidence. Its output records the input editions and the premises discharged. No hidden current-price lookup, host cache, previous standing or optional terminal preference may supply a missing input or select a result.

**Decision (Complete cause boundary)** · `dec:design:public-inputs` Represent a proposed boundary as a closed, inspectable public-input object before computation. Check its accepted-act inventory, referenced history, settled burn samples and effective declarations separately from numerical certification; the publication contains the causes and the evidence needed to repeat both checks. Realizes (`req:vision:public-inputs`); grounded in ``post:network:host-contract``, ``prop:deployment:allocation-input-completeness``.

**Convention (Claim levels)** · `conv:design:claim-levels` Structural validity, certified numerical safety, faithful publication, independent reproduction and operational assurance are separate claims. A passing envelope or matching replay cannot promote one into another. A supported profile states precisely which claims have executed evidence and which remain held (`conv:vision:evidence-ledger`).

## Architecture and data flow · `sec:design:architecture`

**Model (Crate graph)** · `model:design:crates` The adopted domain graph contains 7 crates, with the direct prerequisites below taken from (`conv:decomposition:dependencies`). Every domain name must occur in the concept responsibility map; the infrastructure column includes the accepted amendment (`amend:decomposition:publication`).

| Crate | Responsibility | Direct domain prerequisites | Direct infrastructure prerequisites |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| pn-records | Immutable public inputs: identities, acts, ledger/burn data and declared constants | None | None |
| pn-graph | Structural formation, shared graph algebra and title folds | pn-records | None |
| pn-epoch | Compile and certify one final epoch state and its admission result | pn-records, pn-graph | None |
| pn-readouts | Expose published standing/title and optional exact raw views to terminal consumers | pn-records, pn-graph, pn-epoch | None |
| pn-host | Encode the complete seven-part public package | pn-records, pn-graph, pn-epoch | pn-interchange |
| pn-replay | Reconstruct and compare the binding package from public inputs | pn-records, pn-graph, pn-epoch, pn-host | None |
| pn-verification | Maintain labelled integrated fixtures and independent public-surface checks | pn-records, pn-graph, pn-epoch, pn-readouts, pn-host, pn-replay | None |

**Model (Boundary pipeline)** · `model:design:pipeline` Records enter graph formation and dependent-closure validation, then title reconstruction. The epoch compiler freezes the completed ledger, cells, recipients, base allocation and emission; certification selects a rung, the solve encloses its equilibrium, and admission checks the final state. Host publication encodes that state and its causes. Replay starts from those causes and reconstructs each stage. Readouts consume the immutable final snapshot. Verification consumes public surfaces as a development-only leaf (`dec:decomposition:crates`).

**Invariant (Stage ownership)** · `inv:design:stage-ownership` Records own carriers and validation of their local invariants; graph owns structural meaning; epoch owns binding computation; host owns encoding; replay owns reconstruction and comparison. An upstream crate never calls a downstream crate. Optional raw payloads are passed to host as explicitly nonbinding data, so host has no dependency on the readout engine.

**Model (State progression)** · `model:design:state-progression` An unchecked proposal becomes a structurally validated candidate, then a frozen compiled epoch, then a certified equilibrium, then an admitted final state. Each transition returns its evidence or a typed failure. A candidate is never exposed as accepted merely because an intermediate stage succeeded; only the admitted object can authorize a host publication labelled accepted.

**Invariant (Stable references)** · `inv:design:stable-references` Cross-crate references name structural objects and effective editions, never addresses in mutable storage. Canonical sorting is a presentation rule distinct from authoritative act positions. Collection iteration order cannot break title ties, choose a recipient or choose a rung. Acceleration indexes may be rebuilt from the immutable input without changing its meaning.

## Records and declarations · `sec:design:records`

**Signature (Records)** · `sig:design:records` pn-records offers validate-record, resolve-public-reference, assemble-boundary-inputs and select-effective-declaration. Constructors preserve authored identity, projection completeness, units and vintage; they refuse ambiguous duplicate identities, unresolved required references and invalid local representations. They do not certify formation, destruction of burn backing or a standing result. The same carriers are usable by host and replay without a codec dependency.

**Model (Accepted act)** · `model:design:accepted-act` An act carries its structural identifier, author, family, structural body, mandatory and removable projections, immutable protocol references, authentication evidence, finalization epoch and authoritative position. Edge identifiers derive from the parent act and the declared binary or A/T role. The act owns its full projection set; co-legs cannot appear as independent accounting units. Tombstonable dependency projections and non-tombstonable protocol references remain distinct fields.

**Decision (Whole-act identity)** · `dec:design:complete-acts` Validate and retain every accepted authored act before folding. Keep the member identities of every fold cell as witnesses, but maintain action counts and debit increments from the complete authored-act inventory. A cancelled stance, hostile act, zero coefficient or home recipient never removes that act from accounting. Realizes (`req:vision:complete-acts`); grounded in ``def:graph:act-identifier``, ``def:graph:act-edge-projection``, ``post:epoch:universal-act-weighing``.

**Model (Burn boundary)** · `model:design:burn` The burn carrier contains the value map sampled for every read address, opaque address space, bootstrap capacity bound and unit-cost projection. A companion evidence record carries settlement depth, provenance and the conditional operator bound with its proof when applicable. Both carry their effective edition and boundary sample convention; an absent required disclosure is a publication failure even when no arithmetic formula reads it (`dec:decomposition:burn`).

**Decision (Imported value and audit evidence)** · `dec:design:burn-boundary` Keep the mathematical burn interface separate from evidence about its realization. Verify sample coverage and declared units at the boundary, carry required disclosures unmodified, and report the assurance supplied by the deployment without claiming that a numeric computation proves destruction. Realizes (`req:vision:burn-boundary`); grounded in ``def:comparator:burn-interface``, ``subsec:deployment:realization-obligations``.

**Model (Ledger records)** · `model:design:ledger` Each anchored Actor has a settled cumulative burn value, nonnegative final residual balance, positive action count and a history of charged increments indexed by effective epoch. A proposed increment names the charged value in force and its representation edition. Residual balance is derived from settled burn and consummated debit history, with the proposed epoch increments applied once; published balances are checked outputs, not independent replacement inputs.

**Model (Declarations)** · `model:design:declarations` An effective declaration identifies the formula edition, constant definitions, permitted overrides, announcement and effective boundaries, domain profiles, numerical procedure, authentication realization, publication profile and calibration procedure. Exact literals, computed quantities and illustrative displays have distinct roles. Resolution at an old boundary must return the old declaration, even when a newer one is available.

**Decision (Effective formulas and constants)** · `dec:design:constants` Select declarations by their published effective boundary, reject locked overrides and missing lead, and retain reproduction evidence for computed constants. Generate defaults only through the read-only correspondence gate of the constants bite; compare defining formulas and exact literals separately from certified computed values and displayed rounding. Realizes (`req:vision:constants`); grounded in ``subsec:deployment:transparency-of-protocol-formulas``, ``rem:verification:numerical-rounding``.

**Invariant (Charged value)** · `inv:design:charged-value` The deployment publishes a single charged value for an epoch. Debit, W1, the safety floor, emission and capacity read that same value. The defining calibration formula specifies its target; certification must establish the required conditions for the actual charge. Neither replay nor a price change may reprice a consummated vintage (`dec:decomposition:numbers`).

**Decision (Preserved charges)** · `dec:design:ledger-vintages` The initial supported ledger profile uses exact finite rational charged values with explicit units and certified calibration evidence. Preserve those exact values and their vintages. A profile admitting other exact real descriptions must supply a total interpretation, certified enclosure evaluator and equality-witness contract before support is claimed; a rounded display is never such a description. Realizes (`req:vision:ledger-vintages`); grounded in ``post:epoch:act-debit``, ``def:epoch:safety-threshold``.

## Formation, geometry and title · `sec:design:graph`

**Signature (Graph)** · `sig:design:graph` pn-graph offers validate-formation, project-complete-act, partition-dependencies, evaluate-geometry, recognize-settlements and fold-title. Outputs include checked projection witnesses, structural folds, geometry enclosures and boundary-indexed title evidence. It guarantees the family census, identifier algebra, endpoint typing and reference rules, while leaving standing allocation to epoch and authentication realization to the declared adapter.

**Algorithm (Formation)** · `alg:design:formation` Validate the identifier constructor and family census, endpoint sorts, structural body and complete projection set; verify author approval and permitted projection removal through the declared authentication operation; resolve mandatory public references and check dependency validity at each authoritative position. Registration anchors its existing Actor/Profile pair rather than minting a new pair. A dangling identifier may be syntactically valid without becoming an anchored standing recipient. The governing surfaces are ``def:graph:identifier-algebra``, ``def:graph:verified-act`` and ``def:graph:act-edge-projection``.

**Algorithm (Dependent partition)** · `alg:design:dependency-partition` Against fixed pre-epoch history, compute each proposed act's principal dependency closure, including required co-legs; reject missing dependencies and invalid authoritative placement. Form an intersection graph of these closures and union each connected component. Publish the resulting disjoint blocks and compare their union with the proposed membership. Blocks may interleave in authoritative order; earlier processing cannot discharge another block's obligations. This implements ``def:graph:dependent-act-set``.

**Algorithm (Geometry)** · `alg:design:geometry` Apply the census-defined coordinate extraction, structural-field exclusions, slices and tensor construction in the prescribed order, then derive determinant, sign and parity from the canonical path view. Exact coordinate algebra remains exact where closed; nonlinear kernels return certified enclosures. An unresolved determinant sign is indecisive, never an arbitrary routing polarity. Geometry follows ``post:nodes:canonical-tensor-pipeline`` rather than a generic adjacency approximation.

**Algorithm (Recognition)** · `alg:design:recognition` Enumerate complete Bid/Accept/Ratify candidates with public references to the same Bid and the required exact Accept. Check prior-boundary ownership, the reference-forced handshake chain, and buyer/seller cancellations by finalization epoch; a cancellation at or before Ratify's epoch defeats it. Co-epoch conflicts read membership and epoch, not residual act positions. Recognition implements ``def:graph:settlement-recognition`` independently of sentiment.

**Algorithm (Title fold)** · `alg:design:title` Starting from genesis title, process boundaries in epoch order. No recognized unchallenged candidate leaves title unchanged; a unique candidate transfers title; conflicting candidates are all consumed without transfer. Persist consumption so failed candidates cannot found later recognition, and distinguish ordinary description updates from orphaned title claims. The fold and its prior-owner witnesses implement ``thm:graph:epoch-title-fold`` and ``def:graph:settlement-consumption``.

**Decision (Structural meaning before allocation)** · `dec:design:formation-title` Produce formation and title evidence before recipient resolution. Accept and Ratify allocate outward only when mandatory folded coordinates are positive and their recognized, unchallenged settlement actually transfers title; otherwise they resolve home. Retain successful transfer events across later epochs, and never let a sentiment change rewrite title or terminal membership policy substitute for recognition. Realizes (`req:vision:formation-title`); grounded in ``def:graph:identifier-algebra``, ``def:network:admission-closure``, ``def:graph:act-edge-projection``, ``thm:graph:epoch-title-fold``, ``post:epoch:settlement-standing-title-coupling``, ``def:graph:settlement-recognition``.

**Invariant (Settlement standing)** · `inv:design:settlement-standing` The compiler consumes the title fold's event evidence, not a separate inference from an Accept edge or current owner alone. This preserves event-persistent settlement contributions while later title changes remain possible. Formation order is retained where required; valid dependency permutations cannot manufacture another title or standing answer (`req:vision:formation-title`).

## Epoch compilation and conserved solve · `sec:design:epoch`

**Signature (Epoch)** · `sig:design:epoch` pn-epoch offers compile-final-state, evaluate-rung, certify-equilibrium, assess-admission and derive-next-schedules. Compilation returns a frozen object with complete projection evidence; certification returns a proved enclosure and rung report; admission returns a final-set verdict. Each operation reads the same immutable ledger and declarations and exposes its premises, numerical limits and attribution paths.

**Model (Frozen epoch)** · `model:design:frozen-epoch` The compiled object contains epoch and boundary evidence, ordered accepted-act candidates, the complete final edge set, derived title events, effective declarations, settled burn view, final ledger and increments, fold cells and membership witnesses, recipients, profiles, sparse base rows, source emission, support extrema and rate hull. Standing and admission are absent until their certified transitions complete. Changing any binding field requires a new compilation.

**Algorithm (Ledger completion)** · `alg:design:ledger` Apply every proposed authored-act increment and the price in force to the settled boundary ledger; reject insolvency before solving. Compute final counts, balances and reduced rates with explicit numeraire, preserving all historical charges. Anchored sources have positive counts because Registration is an accepted act. W1 checks the debit accounting and solvency evidence, never incoming transported balance (``post:epoch:act-debit``).

**Algorithm (Fold cells)** · `alg:design:cells` For netting families, partition complete acts by equality of author, family, source, target and full incidence; aggregation-excluded acts become singleton cells. Fold mandatory coordinates and take the geometric mean of their magnitudes once per cell. No mandatory authored coordinates gives coefficient one; exact zero gives coefficient zero without erasing members. Invitation reads folded A-leg u and f only, while its T-leg relevance remains raw context. These are ``def:epoch:standing-fold-cell`` and ``def:epoch:effective-act-contribution``.

**Algorithm (Recipients)** · `alg:design:recipients` Resolve each cell using the paper's family table over anchored Actors. Positive eligible person-directed cells may allocate outward; settlement cells read transfer evidence, Invitation reads revocation, and artifact/control or nonpositive cells resolve home. Revocation uses the latest represented epoch, with revocation winning ties and reinstatement requiring a complete Invitation in a strictly later epoch. This follows ``def:epoch:standing-recipient-resolution`` and ``def:epoch:inviter-revocation``.

**Algorithm (Base allocation)** · `alg:design:base` Start each source row with the positive self-retention base, then add each cell's coefficient weighted by its declared family profile and effective domain weights at its resolved recipient. Validate nonnegative profile entries, unit sum and projection-domain support before accumulation; do not infer an equal split. Keep a sparse ordered row with an explicit diagonal and witnesses to all cells. Normalize only when constructing hop matrices under ``def:epoch:domain-weighted-base-allocation``.

**Decision (Frozen compiler inputs)** · `dec:design:final-state` Compile once from completed membership, final ledger, derived title, effective constants and the declared profile rule. Freeze base allocation and emission throughout certification and solve. Keep authoritative order for formation checks, but make cell construction and standing independent of dependency-compliant permutations when the specified inputs stay fixed. Realizes (`req:vision:final-state`); grounded in ``post:epoch:standing-epoch-set-determinism``, ``post:epoch:standing-memorylessness``, ``prop:deployment:allocation-input-completeness``, ``def:epoch:standing-domain-profile``.

**Algorithm (Emission and support)** · `alg:design:emission-support` Compute source emission as the lesser of unity and final balance divided by charged price times action count. Zero-balance sources retain identity rows. Propagate contributing rate extrema through sparse positive base support at each supported depth, respecting source emission and retained diagonal mass; this derives contributing widths before the solve. Support follows the declared depth mass and ``prop:dynamics:support-state-independence``; an enclosure unable to decide a required support predicate must be refined or reported indecisive.

**Algorithm (Transport evaluation)** · `alg:design:transport` For a candidate standing vector, evaluate the safety-wall-clamped activation and hop-dependent tilt, normalize each sparse hop row and apply products in source-adjacent order. Mix the supported products by normalized depth mass, then apply source emission once and add retained identity mass. Transport the balance/count pair and form the reduced mediant with a proved positive denominator. Never damp inside a hop or scale base scores before normalization as a substitute for emission (``def:epoch:finite-depth-conserved-transport``).

**Decision (Conserved pair operator)** · `dec:design:conservation` Publish the complete factorized emitted operator and verify row-stochasticity, pair-mass totals, the export bound and closed-cluster identities. Standing is a ratio of transported pairs; neither its sum nor a transfer of ledger assets is a conservation claim. A sparse implementation is compared against a dense independent small-case operator including retained diagonal mass. Realizes (`req:vision:conservation`); grounded in ``def:epoch:conserved-standing-equilibrium``, ``post:epoch:standing-pair-mass-conservation``, ``rem:epoch:transport-is-not-ledger-transfer``, ``rem:gates:standing-safety-scope``.

**Algorithm (Width fence)** · `alg:design:fence` Evaluate the paper's rival factor times wall-gain cap, cumulative tilt at the deepest supported horizon and contributing width with outward bounds. Compare its margined value with unity. Equal contributing width at fixed wall, profile and rung yields the same fence regardless of graph shape; an interval evaluation of a tighter graph-sensitive Jacobian supremum is not a replacement certificate (``def:dynamics:conserved-standing-certificate``).

**Decision (Published finite rung search)** · `dec:design:rung-verdicts` Visit the published finite grid greatest first, independently evaluating every attempted rung using the declared deterministic refinement budget. Record PASS, FAIL or INDECISIVE and the deciding evidence, then select the first proved pass. An indecisive higher rung may be passed over only as the published procedure specifies, never renamed FAIL; the exact zero-tilt anchor supplies the terminal certifying rung. Realizes (`req:vision:rung-verdicts`); grounded in ``def:epoch:tilt-backoff-grid``, ``rem:epoch:certificate-failure-versus-indecisive-enclosure``.

**Algorithm (Rung record)** · `alg:design:rungs` The report names the grid edition, ordered attempted rungs, each fence enclosure, precision/refinement steps, budget and verdict, plus the accepted rung. Replay executes the same policy; extra private precision cannot select a different published rung. A local interruption that prevents executing the declared policy produces an incomplete computation, not a fabricated INDECISIVE outcome for an unexecuted rung.

**Algorithm (Point solve)** · `alg:design:solve` Use deterministic fixed-point iteration from a declared point inside the invariant hull, applying the frozen conserved map at the accepted rung. A warm start may accelerate private work only if the declared publication procedure still produces the same canonical evidence and result. Candidate iterates are proposals to the checker, not public standing. The zero-tilt state-independent case is evaluated directly.

**Algorithm (Residual enclosure)** · `alg:design:residual` Certify an upper residual r for a point inside the hull and an upper contraction factor c strictly below unity. The fixed point lies within r divided by one minus c in the infinity norm; intersect that box with the invariant hull. Use outward arithmetic throughout, including the division, and retain the residual, c and hull premises. Refine until all binding comparisons are decided or the declared budget returns INDECISIVE under ``lem:dynamics:fixed-point-residual-enclosure``.

**Decision (Certificate before result)** · `dec:design:certificate` Accept a standing object only with a certified width fence, invariant hull, strict contraction witness and residual enclosure. Use the margined fence to establish the theorem premises, not the solver iteration count or an approximate residual. Require independent numerical and adversarial boundary evidence before claiming any supported nonlinear profile trustworthy. Realizes (`req:vision:certificate`); grounded in ``def:dynamics:conserved-standing-certificate``, ``thm:dynamics:certified-conserved-standing-uniqueness``, ``lem:dynamics:fixed-point-residual-enclosure``.

## Final membership, admission and schedules · `sec:design:admission`

**Decision (Checked final union)** · `dec:design:final-membership` Accept a proposed authoritative sequence only with author approvals, formation and dependency evidence at its positions, a disjoint dependent-set partition covering its union, target-size compliance, closure cause and attested boundary timestamp. Check the published earliest target/minimum-duration or maximum-duration rule against supplied boundary evidence; selection among valid sequences remains the host responsibility. Realizes (`req:vision:final-membership`); grounded in ``def:network:write``, ``post:epoch:final-act-sequence-closure``, ``post:network:host-contract``.

**Algorithm (Admission)** · `alg:design:admission` On the completed certified state, check W1 from own-balance debit accounting, W2a for every act's author against the reduced safety floor, and W2b from the act-weighted epoch stamp against the effective participation floor and target capacity. Compute headroom using the same increments and reduced stamps. Preserve individual W2a failure even when the epoch average passes; W2b is vacuous for an empty epoch. No prefix or partial solve has normative standing (``post:epoch:final-act-sequence-write-rule``).

**Decision (Atomic final admission)** · `dec:design:admission` Require every structural, solvency, safety, policy and publication prerequisite to pass for the proposed final union. Refine a threshold-straddling enclosure or reject the proposal as uncertified with its INDECISIVE reason retained. Publish no accepted standing and apply no member of a rejected or unfinished proposed set. Realizes (`req:vision:admission`); grounded in ``post:epoch:final-act-sequence-write-rule``, ``post:network:host-contract``, ``def:epoch:final-act-stamps``.

**Invariant (Atomic admission)** · `inv:design:atomic-admission` Admission returns an immutable admitted state only after all checks complete. Failed candidates may produce diagnostic evidence, explicitly marked as proposed and nonbinding, but cannot advance the final edge set or consume a debit. Persistence and consensus integrations must preserve that transition when they are separately designed.

**Algorithm (Schedules)** · `alg:design:schedules` Execute the deployment's total calibration procedure on the stated certificate and boundary snapshot, compute the next charged value and certify X1–X7 for that charge, including width dominance and strict contraction margin. Validate the announced policy floor against the led safety floor with explicit numeraire and effective boundary. An understated realized width invalidates the affected certification; a step cap cannot rescue it by silently clipping an unsafe increase (``def:epoch:safety-threshold``).

**Decision (Total calibration declaration)** · `dec:design:policy-schedules` Treat the released Q20 correction as a deployment obligation to publish a total procedure, including zero width, empty live domain, trigger/cap/escalation order, calibration window, next-width failure and snapshot rules. Preserve the chartered target, constants, lead and no-silent-change constraints. Withhold the dependent admission claim when the declaration or its evidence is absent; do not hold it for an already supplied paper-maintainer answer. Realizes (`req:vision:policy-schedules`); grounded in ``def:epoch:safety-threshold``, ``def:epoch:policy-floor``, ``subsec:deployment:transparency-of-protocol-formulas``.

**Invariant (Unit boundary)** · `inv:design:units` Carriers distinguish burn, burn per action, action count, full standing and reduced standing. Reduction names its numeraire explicitly; stamps and headroom are reduced, while debit and capacity consume the charged burn price. Tests vary numeraire under the declared covariance and never repair an inconsistent fixture by an unrecorded rescaling.

## Numerical design · `sec:design:numbers`

**Model (Exact values and enclosures)** · `model:design:numbers` Structural identifiers, counts, multiplicities, positions and epoch indices are exact discrete values. Finite declared ledger values and rational constants use normalized arbitrary-precision rationals within declared bit limits. Nonlinear graph kernels, activation, gain inversion, powers and equilibrium values use certified closed intervals with rational endpoints. An interval denotes knowledge about a value, never a substitute charged ledger amount.

**Signature (Numeric kernel)** · `sig:design:numeric-kernel` The records layer owns exact scalar carriers and the shared numerical contract; graph implements the reusable kernel, and epoch composes its operations. The kernel offers checked exact arithmetic, interval arithmetic, elementary-function enclosure, sign/comparison and witness checking. Every operation declares its domain, outward-rounding guarantee, precision budget and failure mode. No operation returns an ordinary scalar when its only justified result is an enclosure.

**Rule (Backend evidence)** · `rule:design:backend-evidence` Before the numeric bite, review the chosen backend's directed rounding, arbitrary-precision integer conversion, elementary-function bounds, branch domains and platform behavior. Pin versions and record independent oracle vectors, including underflow/overflow paths and extreme cancellation. The contract permits an interval wrapper over a reviewed backend; naming a library or increasing point precision alone discharges no soundness obligation (`open:design:numeric-backend`).

**Algorithm (Enclosure composition)** · `alg:design:enclosures` Convert exact rationals outward at the requested precision, enclose primitive results, and propagate intervals through the specified expression graph. Prove denominator separation from zero before division and domain validity before logarithms, roots or powers. Use monotonicity only with checked domain premises; splitting an interval is permitted when the union still covers the full input. Invalid mathematical input differs from a valid input whose finite enclosure cannot decide a branch.

**Rule (Comparison)** · `rule:design:numeric-comparison` For a required non-strict x greater-than-or-equal-to y comparison, PASS requires a lower bound of x at least the upper bound of y, and FAIL requires an upper bound of x below the lower bound of y. Reverse the endpoints for less-than-or-equal-to. Otherwise refine or return INDECISIVE. Strict comparisons use the corresponding strict separation; a certificate must prove c strictly below unity before residual division.

**Rule (Equality witnesses)** · `rule:design:equality` Equality is decidable for normalized rationals and identical canonical exact expressions within their declared interpretation. Other equalities require a checked algebraic or analytic witness supported by the profile; intersecting intervals, equal rounded displays and solver stagnation are not equality proofs. A boundary equality may pass a non-strict gate only through exact arithmetic or a reviewed witness. Unsupported witness forms report unsupported, and unresolved supported forms report INDECISIVE.

**Rule (Deterministic refinement)** · `rule:design:refinement` The numerical declaration fixes the starting precision, refinement sequence, operation and iteration caps, expression evaluation order, stopping predicates and output endpoint grid. The reference solve starts from the declaration's hull point and records every binding stopping decision. Reproducibility applies to that finite procedure; a verifier with a smaller local resource allowance reports unsupported instead of choosing a different stopping point.

**Verification (Adversarial numerics)** · `verif:design:numerical-boundaries` Before enabling the kernel, exercise exact equality, one representable value on either side of each gate, intervals straddling a wall, zero width, extreme rational size, small positive denominators, gain inversion near its domain edge and exhausted refinement. Check containment against an independent directed computation and exact analytic cases. These fixtures settle the finite decision contract; they do not prove a backend by sampling alone.

**Open Question (Numerical implementation evidence)** · `open:design:numeric-backend` Owner: the numeric-bite implementer and reviewer. The abstract exact/enclosure/equality contracts are fixed, but the backend, version pins, native requirements and soundness dossier remain held. Settlement requires reviewed primitive guarantees and execution of (`verif:design:numerical-boundaries`) with an independent oracle before nonlinear claims are accepted. This is the remaining evidence dependency of (`open:vision:numerical-evidence`), not a permission to use unchecked point arithmetic.

## Verdicts and failures · `sec:design:verdicts`

**Model (Verdict algebra)** · `model:design:verdicts` A checked proposition returns PASS with premises and evidence, FAIL with a proved countercondition, or INDECISIVE with the unresolved comparison and exhausted declared refinement. Admission additionally reports accepted or not accepted, preserving the proposition verdicts that explain it. A failed rung is distinct from a failed proposal; a proposal can be not accepted because its evidence is indecisive without a proof that its exact standing is unsafe.

**Model (Operational failures)** · `model:design:failures` Malformed input reports the field and violated representation rule; unsupported reports an unknown profile, operation, witness or preflight size; resource exhaustion reports the stage, declared limit and progress; interruption and internal invariant failure report no mathematical verdict. An unfinished operation cannot create PASS or FAIL. Each report names the affected structural object, epoch, claim and evidence path.

**Rule (Cross-crate propagation)** · `rule:design:failure-propagation` Preserve the originating category and attribution when crossing a crate boundary. A downstream stage may refuse to proceed but may not turn an upstream INDECISIVE into FAIL or a failed decoder into a mathematical rejection. Aggregation retains individual failures, and a final certificate is unavailable while any required premise is unproved.

**Convention (Interchange verdicts)** · `conv:design:interchange-verdicts` Canonical decoding, interchange acceptance, supported application semantics and mathematical verification are separate report fields. A tolerant envelope acceptance under (`def:interchange:acceptance`) does not prove unknown binding semantics; report unsupported full verification when necessary while retaining the interchange verdict. First-party invocation streams follow (`rec:output:decision`), and externally consumed artifacts follow (`rec:interchange:decision`).

## Publication, encoding and replay · `sec:design:publication`

**Signature (Host)** · `sig:design:host` pn-host offers assemble-publication, validate-package-completeness, encode-canonical and decode-canonical under its public profile. Assembly accepts admitted final state, the closed public-input object, effective declarations and required evidence. It checks agreement among section references and refuses to label an incomplete or unaccepted candidate as a final package. It neither selects membership nor recomputes standing to repair missing evidence.

**Model (Publication sections)** · `model:design:package` The package has 7 mandatory semantic sections under ``post:network:host-contract``. The proposed content-key allocation below is local design data, not a paper-mandated byte schema; keys must be distinct, above the envelope keys and allocated in the stated order. The complete factorized operator suffices without its dense expansion (Q22 in (`reg:questions:overview`)).

| 3 | final-ledger | Settled burn view, residual balances, final action counts, increments and charged debit vintages, preserving units and effective boundaries. |

**Protocol (Encoding profile)** · `proto:design:encoding` The envelope uses namespace at key 0 and version at key 1; the section allocation is (`model:design:package`). Intervals are ordered pairs of such rationals. **Rule (Structural ordering)** · `rule:design:ordering` Encode structural identifiers as tagged constructor records with fixed field positions, then order unordered Actor and support collections by their canonical structural encodings. **Decision (Attributable independent reconstruction)** · `dec:design:replay` Separate complete input inventory from numerical safety, recompute the binding package and compare by the declared contract. **Rule (Replay comparison)** · `rule:design:comparison` Exact normalized records, chosen rung, verdicts, effective declarations and canonical output of the same finite procedure compare exactly. **Invariant (Identity and authentication)** · `inv:design:identity` Hashes may support a declared authentication realization subject to (`rec:identity:decision`), but do not replace structural identity, select among valid mathematical objects or substitute for replay. ## Deployment declaration surface · `sec:design:deployment`

**Model (Profile contract)** · `model:design:profile` A profile is a public versioned declaration whose procedures have deterministic interpretation, explicit domains and finite execution policy. **Setting (Q03)** · `setup:design:charged-value` Declare charged representation, practical accuracy, units and certification for the value actually debited; historical vintage identity. **Setting (Q04)** · `setup:design:publication-codec` Declare canonical ordering, numeric representation, schema edition, rounding disclosure and replay comparison. Closed deployment choice; this implementation offers the accepted CBOR profile, without calling it the paper codec.

**Setting (Q05)** · `setup:design:certification` Declare finite rung order, deterministic precision sequence, operation budgets, equality witnesses, residual stopping and each indecision outcome. Closed deployment choice; choose the greatest rung this procedure proves passing, retaining higher INDECISIVE results.

**Setting (Q07)** · `setup:design:authentication` Declare proposal binding, author approval, allowed projection removal and public protocol reference preservation; adapter edition and verification material. **Setting (Q15)** · `setup:design:domain-profiles` Declare family-indexed nonnegative unit-sum profiles supported on projection domains, together with effective domain weights. **Setting (Q20)** · `setup:design:calibration` Declare positive zero-width and empty-domain behavior, trigger/cap/escalation order, calibration window, next-width failure rule and boundary snapshot convention. **Rule (Profile validation)** · `rule:design:profile-validation` Validate all declaration fields and effective boundaries before compiling a candidate. Reject a missing family profile, unannounced override, undefined calibration branch or changed locked value as an invalid declaration. A valid but unsupported representation or procedure reports unsupported. ## Risks, reconciliation and open questions · `sec:design:questions`

**Resolution (Design reconciliation)** · `resol:design:reconciliation` (`open:vision:design-reconciliation`) is settled as an explicit compatibility account: the semantic package remains complete, deployment choices remain declared, older calibration holds follow the released status, and external encoding follows interchange through the accepted proposals (`proposal:design:encoding`) and (`proposal:design:interchange-dependency`). Record 007 carries their acceptance in (`amend:decomposition:publication`); completion of the byte profile remains the publication-bite obligation (`open:design:publication-profile`).

**Resolution (Evidence coverage)** · `resol:design:coverage` (`open:vision:evidence-coverage`) is settled at the planning level by (`ledger:design:evidence`): every concept requirement has fixtures, properties, an independent-oracle route and a stated hold. Execution coverage remains held until the corresponding bites populate that ledger with actual results; no domain fixture is represented as already passing.

**Resolution (Numerical evidence)** · `resol:design:numerical-evidence` (`open:vision:numerical-evidence`) is settled for the public contract by exact carriers, checked enclosures, residual evidence, equality witnesses and explicit indecision. Trust in a concrete supported backend remains held at (`open:design:numeric-backend`), including independent adversarial runs. The first records bite may establish structural carriers without claiming those nonlinear operations implemented.

**Warning (False confidence)** · `warn:design:false-confidence` The design response to (`warn:vision:false-confidence`) separates completeness, safety and independent evidence in (`rule:design:independence`) and requires act-omission and correlated-error challenges. Residual confidence remains held until those checks execute and their independence is reviewed; a shared-kernel replay match cannot close it. Owner: the verification-bite maintainer and audit reviewer.

**Warning (Performance)** · `warn:design:performance` The response to (`warn:vision:performance`) is the declared workload, limit and measurement contract. Feasibility remains held because no domain implementation has measured those workloads. Owner: each bite's implementer, with the commissioning reviewer accepting the resulting deployment budget; settlement requires (`expt:design:measurement-gate`) without relaxing exact semantics.

**Open Question (Proposal acceptance)** · `open:design:proposal-review` Settled by the repository owner's acceptance, recorded with its reasons in (`amend:decomposition:publication`), (`dec:decomposition:encoding`) and (`conv:decomposition:dependencies`). Owner: repository maintainers. The encoding replacement and pn-host infrastructure dependency preserve the semantic package, domain boundaries and bite order; no proposal-acceptance hold remains. The separate profile-completion obligation is (`open:design:publication-profile`).

**Open Question (Deployment instantiation)** · `open:design:deployment-profile` Owner: the deployment. Supply public effective choices for (`model:design:profile`), including a total calibration procedure and supported numerical policy, then demonstrate their constraints on exact boundary fixtures. Until then the design supports contracts and reference cases but cannot certify a concrete deployment. This preserves (`open:vision:deployment-profile`) and (`open:vision:operational-adoption`).

**Open Question (Residual normative conflict)** · `open:design:paper-conflict` Owner: the paper's maintainers when a conflicting pair of released claims is identified. Record both labels and premises, hold only the affected claim, and require clarification or a released correction. This escalation route follows (`open:vision:paper-conflict`); historical DEFECT classifications alone do not open new holds after their corrected Status. Any concept or record defect discovered in later implementation is reported for its owner rather than repaired through implementation behavior.

## Design gate · `sec:design:gate`

**Gate (Before the first domain bite)** · `gate:design:domain-entry` Review and accept the concept-to-design coverage, domain contracts, frozen-state model, verdict algebra and scoped holds. Every requirement must have a realizing decision and evidence plan; every known unsupported choice must identify the dependent claim and owner. The first structural records bite may proceed with later numerical and publication evidence held only where its carriers preserve the contracts and make no unsupported claim.

**Gate (Dependency readiness)** · `gate:design:dependency-readiness` Before the affected bite, resolve its deployment declaration, numeric backend or publication profile gate and transcribe its independent fixtures. Do not lock a charged-value encoding without its interpretation and certification contract, execute calibration without a total published procedure, or claim external byte compatibility before the assigned theory and vectors exist. These are bite-specific prerequisites, not a reason to disguise unfinished evidence as complete.

**Gate (Document integrity)** · `gate:design:document-integrity` Render all inputs and documents to a fixed point, check every repository citation and environment kind, and pass the repository gate under (`gate:foundation:verification`). The coverage premises must fail on a missing requirement mapping or unrendered traced decision. These checks establish a reviewable design and its correspondence; the later implementation evidence gate remains (`gate:design:implementation-evidence`).
