## Identity and signing in an epoch · `sec:author:meaning`

**Convention (Actor)** · `conv:author:actor` The author is the Actor, permanently identified by the token's asset identifier qualified by the deployment's run (`def:identity:author-token`). The token keeps that identity fixed while the keys authorized to sign for it can change. Creating a token is not Registration, standing, title, human identity or uniqueness of a person (`amend:identity:author-token-binding`).

**Convention (Control)** · `conv:author:control` Control of the output holding the token permits its next supported move and the declaration of signing delegates; it identifies no person and is not private custody. Supported means within the declared token forms whose path the deployment can follow publicly (`rule:devnet:author-token`). A delegate can sign for the Actor without becoming its identity or gaining control of the token (`amend:identity:author-token-binding`).

**Convention (Epoch delegate set)** · `conv:author:epoch-set` For a given epoch, follow the token's path in that epoch's fixed settled chain prefix. Its delegate set is the union of the valid declarations from the cutoff in force onward, including the declarations at the cutoff. The cutoff starts at qualified issuance and incorporates the effective points encoded by the Actor's own acts in earlier epochs. A move can add declarations; a move without declarations preserves the set. Movement alone retires none (`rule:identity:author-key-history`), (`rule:identity:author-chain-view`), (`rule:identity:retirement-order`). **Convention (Cutoff effect)** · `conv:author:cutoff` Every act of an epoch uses that epoch's delegate set, even when it encodes a new cutoff. The latest valid non-backward point along the token's path named by that Actor's ordered acts becomes effective in the following epoch; without such a point the cutoff persists. A well-formed unknown, future or backward point is ineffective, not a deferred instruction; missing evidence is unavailable rather than proof of absence. **Convention (Two checks)** · `conv:author:checks` A signature is checked under the key the record maps to its covered bytes. **Convention (Delegation)** · `conv:author:delegation` The control key is not automatically an act-signing key.
