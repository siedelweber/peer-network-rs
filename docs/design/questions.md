## Premises · `sec:questions:premises`

**Setup (Sources)** · `setup:questions:sources` The register preserves the study's thirty-eight readings alongside the maintainers' classifications and current implementer instructions. The paper release is network-v0.28.0. Study readings and ambiguities remain historical; classifications, instructions and status paragraphs are the released answers. Current Status and normative sections govern over the original Class and study reading. The accepted decomposition supplies the historical questions. Status paragraphs preserve the released meaning and bold marker; positional paper references are replaced by their current labels and renamed paper labels move to their released successors; Q07 and Q22 have no status paragraph in the answers document and no status field here. Section records each question section heading verbatim, and ambiguity preserves the study cell verbatim except for those positional references and renamed labels. The paper retains no compatibility labels before version 1, so these substitutions keep the cited statements resolvable. Study readings remain proposals; a classification or instruction does not itself amend the paper. The record (`rec:decomposition:decision`) retains the implementation holds. No question-to-bite mapping is inferred.

**Convention (Presentation)** · `conv:questions:presentation` The topic and reading retain the study's wording; class and instruction follow the released answers, with positional paper references expanded to their labels. Paper labels are exhibited with doubled delimiters, never minted or cited here. ANSWERED, DEFECT and OWNER-CALL are the maintainers' classifications, not implementation completion states.
