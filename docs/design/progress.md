# Specification realization tracker at network-v0.31.1 · `reg:progress:overview`

**Convention (Citation coverage)** · `conv:progress:citation-coverage` This tracker measures inspectable citation coverage, not the share of the specification that is built. Unassigned means no design record cites the statement's exact paper label. Much implemented behaviour is described by records that cite a group of paper statements without naming every label, so unassigned is a citation-coverage gap, not a claim that nothing implements the statement. Realized means an inspectable route from an exact citation to executed evidence in a named scope. Its count cannot stand for implementation completeness: broader references and other implementation evidence lie outside this exact-label join.

**Convention (Evidence routes)** · `conv:progress:evidence-routes` A statement count is useful only when its route to executed evidence is inspectable. This tracker joins exact inventory labels to labelled design paragraphs harvested by the claim checker. A paper mention is an ordinary PAPER citation or an exact doubled-delimiter inventory label in that paragraph; explanatory words, inventory table rows and this tracker itself create no edges. A claim reaches a record through an explicit test-site citation, or through the checked obligation edge when the record is that obligation. The claim must pass all its registered executions and combined members, and belong to an obligation whose executed result is scoped-passed. The row retains the record, claim and scoped obligation rather than treating a mention as a proof of the whole paper statement. These relations use the checked evidence of (`rule:testing:results`); planned claim names and manually written implementation-status prose supply no evidence.

**Convention (Status precedence)** · `conv:progress:status-precedence` Realized means at least one such scoped execution route. Held means there is a design mention, no realized route, and every mentioning record is a held obligation, a named hold used by a held obligation, or explicitly cites such a hold. Designed means there is a design mention but neither of those conditions holds; an unheld design route prevents held-only classification. Unassigned means there is no exact-label design mention. A statement realized in one scope and held in another counts once as realized, with both routes retained below; this is scoped realization, not universal completion or discharge of the other hold. A failed execution cannot realize a statement and still refuses the gate. Rows whose proposed crate is none form the separate prose total, not implementation completion.

**Convention (Carrier and verification)** · `conv:progress:carrier` The progress carrier is generated by ci/claims.py tracker from source- and configuration-matched checked execution evidence. It is not an authored status register. The documents generator projects that carrier to its dataset and renders these tables without arithmetic. After checking the current executions, the claim-evidence gate recomputes every row and total and requires byte equality with the committed carrier, so a status change cannot leave a green stale table. Regeneration uses the same computation as that check. Removing a claim's evidence changes a realized row to designed when its design mention remains and there is no held-only route. Counts have this dataset as their only carrier and gain no numeric-registry copies.

**Convention (Bite membership)** · `conv:progress:bite-membership` A statement belongs to a bite when one of its mentioning records is a declared bite contract, a declared vision requirement, or a requirement realizer reached through the obligation register's directed realizer edges. No arbitrary prose path is followed. A statement may belong to several bites and is counted once within each; bite totals therefore are not additive. Unlinked names the inventory rows outside those declared edges. Crate and kind partitions each count every inventory row once, independently of bite membership. A bite row reports statement realization, not whether its implementation gate is closed.

## Record citation coverage · `sec:progress:record-coverage`

**Convention (Record attribution)** · `conv:progress:record-attribution` The record table counts labelled paragraphs harvested by the claim checker, excluding this tracker. A package README's records belong to the crate named in that package's Cargo manifest; records in the repository README, ADRs and design documents are repository-wide and counted separately. Existing packages and proposed inventory crates appear even when they have no records. Exact-citing counts records that mention at least one inventory label under the evidence-route convention, once per record regardless of the number of labels. This source-location attribution makes the denominator independent of citation coverage; it does not assign repository-wide records to crates by inference or equate a record with an implemented statement.
