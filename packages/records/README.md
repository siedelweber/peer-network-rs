# Internal records · `guide:acts:records`

**Section (Acts)** · `sec:acts:area-acts` Collapsing parallel identities, admitting partial co-legs or erasing required protocol references changes the complete act set before any domain accounting. The first slice implements local validation under (`[ROOT-sig:design:records]`) and (`[ROOT-model:design:accepted-act]`); it does not certify authentication, formation, membership, family endpoint typing or a standing result.

**Definition (Structural identity)** · `def:acts:identity` An ActId consists of its exact public author identifier, author-local sequence and exact family name. An EdgeId consists of its parent ActId and Binary, A or T role. Authoritative position, finalization epoch and removal facts are not identifier fields. Equal endpoints do not collapse distinct edge identities.

**Definition (References)** · `def:acts:references` PublicReference is an act, edge or exact namespace/name identity. ValidatedHistory derives act and leg references from complete locally validated history acts; separately supplied external names cannot inject an act or edge. A batch identity already in history refuses as a duplicate. resolve_public_reference takes ValidatedRecords and distinguishes History from Batch, refusing missing or malformed queries. Tombstones never erase these identities. No database or codec is consulted.

**Definition (Projection ownership)** · `def:acts:projection` A Binary layout owns one Binary structural leg; a Paired layout owns A and T together. Neither leg owns a removal state: any candidate leg removal field refuses. RemovableProjections carries the act's independent content and dependency facts; a tombstone must follow the act's authoritative position. Dependency edge status derives from this act-owned dependency fact, never from a co-leg. Both structural legs and immutable protocol identities survive a dependency tombstone, following (`[ROOT-amend:design:act-removal-facts]`). This snapshot validator admits no mutation or resurrection operation.

**Definition (Structural body)** · `def:acts:body` The first local body representation declares its projection layout and named exact integer quantities with explicit units and vintages. Validation preserves these fields without conversion and refuses empty or duplicate quantity names and empty units. This bounded integer carrier is not the later family census, rational valuation or external byte profile; those contracts must supply their own types before use. No codec or canonical encoding is assigned here under (`[ROOT-amend:design:bite-one-internal-envelopes]`).

**Definition (Act carrier)** · `def:acts:carrier` CandidateAct is unchecked input: structural identifier, body, candidate legs, act-owned projections, immutable protocol references, public authentication evidence, finalization epoch and authoritative position. ActId alone contains author and family. ValidatedAct, ValidatedLeg, BoundaryInputs and PrincipalClosure have private fields and read-only accessors, so only validation and assembly construct them. ValidatedAct is a distinct owned view with ValidatedLeg values that have no candidate removal slot; dependency_status takes ValidatedRecords and derives an edge's status from its owning act, without storing a second status on the leg. ValidatedRecords exposes borrowed views of its private immutable snapshot, never candidates. Nonempty authentication evidence is retained without certifying authenticity.

**Definition (Local validation)** · `def:acts:validation` validate_record checks a complete candidate batch against ValidatedHistory and opaque declarations, then owns a distinct ValidatedRecords snapshot. Duplicate identities, missing references, missing co-legs, invalid local fields and dependencies return typed ValidationError variants. Validation preserves complete acts, quantities, units, vintages and authoritative positions without an encoding. The unpublished error surface remains exhaustive; InvalidCause, DependencyCause and DeclarationCause distinguish local reasons. Every public operation is counted and timed at every level with an explicit refusal outcome for contract rejection; only trace captures full content. The executable owns bootstrap. The development-only test-support feature exposes the shared finite-DAG generator; default production builds have no codec, graph or testkit dependency.


**Definition (Opaque declarations)** · `def:acts:declarations` DeclarationId contains namespace, name and edition; Declaration retains announcement and effective boundaries without a payload. Duplicate identities and simultaneous successors refuse. Empty namespaces, empty names and late announcements have distinct DeclarationCause values; malformed selection queries refuse as InvalidSelectionKey, independently of edition availability. select_effective_declaration selects by effective boundary rather than edition number or input order, retaining historical answers under (`[ROOT-amend:design:bite-one-declaration-selection]`). Bite 2 confirms or explicitly changes the late-announcement rule; declaration contents remain there.

**Definition (Dependency and parent content)** · `def:acts:dependencies` DependencyProjection has FullEmpty, Full and Tombstoned states. Full contains a nonempty, sorted, duplicate-free, self-free list of act identifiers for placement. StructuralBody.asserted_parents is a separate non-removable, sorted, duplicate-free, self-free Vec of ActId; only it defines the causal-parent relation. Both resolve to strictly earlier acts in history or proposals, never leg identifiers. Their derived order is author bytes, then sequence, then family bytes. MAX_DEPENDENCIES and MAX_ASSERTED_PARENTS are provisional 1024-entry bounds; published count parameters and absent identifier byte bounds remain held for bite 2 under (`[ROOT-amend:design:provisional-carriage-bounds]`). This separates the paper’s plain-mentioned ``def:graph:act-causal-parents`` and ``def:graph:act-dependency-projection``; no paper data is transcribed here.

**Definition (Validated history)** · `def:acts:history` validate_history checks previously accepted whole acts and external names before deriving all act and leg references together. It does not certify host admission or cryptographic evidence. validate_record resolves both relation lists against that fixed history and the proposed batch, with strictly earlier target positions. Earlier proposals remain outside history: processing order cannot enlarge it. The owned snapshot admits no isolated history edge or post-validation mutation.

**Definition (Boundary inputs)** · `def:acts:boundary-inputs` assemble_boundary_inputs takes ValidatedRecords and a position cut: history precedes it and proposals are at or after it. It memoizes each proposal’s asserted-parent ancestors outside history once, preserving overlaps and all co-legs inside whole acts. History parents add nothing and tombstoned dependency carriage has no effect. Every closure includes its root; their union is exactly the proposals. Output follows authoritative position with structural identity breaking presentation ties. Work is an optional exact count of node visits, parent inspections, inherited-member insert attempts and emitted members; overflow is unavailable, never zero. The separate records assembly_steps counter mirrors those steps; work_operations remains one per invocation. Pure field accessors inherit their enclosing operation rather than counting another act access. No partition blocks or exhaustion budget are invented under (`[ROOT-amend:design:boundary-snapshot-representation]`).

**Definition (Shared test support)** · `def:acts:test-support` The test-support feature contains records-dag-1: shrinkable word vectors select layouts, causal parents and placement dependencies independently over two history acts and two to ten proposals, including live and valid tombstoned projection states. The inner public-surface predicates compare closures with a naive transitive search, exact identity sets and work counts, original, reversed and seeded-shuffled presentations, and whole-leg ownership. Nineteen named corruptions cover duplicate identities, missing co-legs, co-leg removal, unresolved references, parent and dependency missing/late/noncanonical/self/over-bound states, history duplicates, isolated history edges and boundary placement. pn-testkit supplies the recorded property configuration and proptest shrinking; pn-verification consumes the same generator through public APIs, with its own fixed-point closure oracle and expected-cause table. The two over-bound mutations also repeat an unresolved identity: they establish count-check precedence over duplicate and missing identities, not isolated invalidity. No independent implementation is claimed.


**Section (Numerics)** · `sec:numerics:area-numerics` Confusing units, market repricing and recoinage can certify the wrong cost or treat a rounded boundary as exact. The pure slice follows (`[ROOT-inv:design:units]`) and (`[ROOT-sig:design:numeric-kernel]`), under (`[ROOT-amend:decomposition:numeric-backend]`) and (`[ROOT-amend:decomposition:numeric-representation]`).

**Definition (Exact carriers)** · `def:numerics:exact-carriers` NumericContext owns explicit CAS resource limits and an accuracy comparison-attempt ledger. Literal retains its original text and canonical exact rational; Computed retains exact arithmetic output; RoundedDisplay is nonbinding candidate text with no conversion to a binding wall. Rational arithmetic never reads a display, metric or clock. The current exact arithmetic subset is rational; the symbolic ridge floor is retained by definition and unresolved comparisons are INDECISIVE. ActionCount cannot be passed to apply_cost, and RoundedDisplay cannot be passed to charged_price; compile-fail examples enforce both distinctions. Pure immutable carrier accessors inherit their producing operation, while every numeric boundary carries full public content at trace and counts outcomes at every level.

**Definition (Dimensioned values)** · `def:numerics:dimensioned-values` BurnAmount, BurnPerAction, ActionCount, FullStanding and ReducedStanding are distinct types. Reduction retains the named positive Numeraire, including its burn unit and exact amount. Recoinage co-scales amounts and numeraire into the new unit; market repricing divides the amount by the positive market factor at fixed purchasing power and leaves the numeraire fixed, so reduced quantities also change inversely. UnitCost is a positive external denomination per named burn unit with public applicable terms, with no universal lower bound; it multiplies BurnAmount only, including the reconstructed sum theta plus numeraire times reduced standing. RecordingEventCost is separate audit evidence. Width fixtures compare declared family inputs; no fence evaluation or universal family law is implemented here.

**Definition (Shared numeric inputs)** · `def:numerics:shared-inputs` The test-support feature adds records-numerics-1: six fixed word-vector cases and a bounded mapping to nonnegative burn and action quantities and positive numeraire and market factors. Both consumers share these inputs and shrinking; each owns its expectations. The inner tier checks exact values and coordinate equalities; pn-verification uses integer cross-products over the bounded input domain. Each property inherits the environment-reading pn-testkit configuration and the consumer's recorded seed. This is independent checking of one implementation, not a second numeric implementation or unrestricted theorem.

**Section (Declarations)** · `sec:declarations:area-declarations` Replacing historical definitions with current constants can change an old boundary answer without changing its identity. This slice supplies payload semantics for (`[ROOT-model:design:declarations]`) and (`[ROOT-model:design:wall-floor]`), without implementing durable history or controller evaluation.

**Definition (Constants edition)** · `def:declarations:constants-edition` ConstantsHistory binds exactly one payload to every validated opaque DeclarationId and resolves through select_effective_declaration. Each payload retains the formula and controller editions, chosen floor, numeraire, source constant definitions, permitted override names, domain profiles, comparison procedure, authentication realization and publication profile. Structural floor is excluded from epoch overrides. Floor explicitly chooses exact 3/37 or the defining ridge image p/(1-p); neither is a default. The ridge variant names the defining real of the paper's ``case:epoch:safety-wall-floor-ridge-floor``, never its rounded root or enclosure endpoints. Exact rational comparison admits equality and greater walls, refuses lesser walls and leaves unresolved or exhausted comparison INDECISIVE; that outcome cannot create a charged price. Symbolic source definitions are retained as source, not certified by evaluating their spelling. Profile and controller identities remain opaque pending their owning bites.

**Section (Ledger vintages)** · `sec:ledger:area-ledger` Revaluing a consummated charge at a later price erases its original debit vintage. The carrier realizes the scoped coordinate requirement of (`[ROOT-inv:design:charged-value]`); accepted burn increments and ledger accounting remain in their later slices.

**Definition (Charged coordinate)** · `def:ledger:charged-coordinate` ChargedPrice privately retains one exact rational wall, its numeraire and their derived burn-per-action price after checking the declared floor. A caller cannot independently set a second price. ChargedVintage retains that pair, action count, boundary and exact charged amount; later price creation has no reference that can mutate it. This is the consummated carrier witness, not a host admission or conserved-ledger implementation.
