# Internal records · `guide:acts:records`

**Section (Acts)** · `sec:acts:area-acts` Collapsing parallel identities, admitting partial co-legs or erasing required protocol references changes the complete act set before any domain accounting. The first slice implements local validation under (`[ROOT-sig:design:records]`) and (`[ROOT-model:design:accepted-act]`); it does not certify authentication, formation, membership, family endpoint typing or a standing result.

**Definition (Structural identity)** · `def:acts:identity` An ActId consists of its exact public author identifier, author-local sequence and exact family name. An EdgeId consists of its parent ActId and Binary, A or T role. Authoritative position, finalization epoch and removal facts are not identifier fields. Equal endpoints do not collapse distinct edge identities.

**Definition (References)** · `def:acts:references` PublicReference is an act, edge or exact namespace/name identity. ValidatedHistory derives act and leg references from complete locally validated history acts; separately supplied external names cannot inject an act or edge. A batch identity already in history refuses as a duplicate. resolve_public_reference takes ValidatedRecords and distinguishes History from Batch, refusing missing or malformed queries. Tombstones never erase these identities. No database or codec is consulted.

**Definition (Projection ownership)** · `def:acts:projection` A Binary layout owns one Binary structural leg; a Paired layout owns A and T together. Neither leg owns a removal state: any candidate leg removal field refuses. RemovableProjections carries the act's independent content and dependency facts; a tombstone must follow the act's authoritative position. Dependency edge status derives from this act-owned dependency fact, never from a co-leg. Both structural legs and immutable protocol identities survive a dependency tombstone, following (`[ROOT-amend:design:act-removal-facts]`). This snapshot validator admits no mutation or resurrection operation.

**Definition (Structural body)** · `def:acts:body` The first local body representation declares its projection layout and named exact integer quantities with explicit units and vintages. Validation preserves these fields without conversion and refuses empty or duplicate quantity names and empty units. This bounded integer carrier is not the later family census, rational valuation or external byte profile; those contracts must supply their own types before use. No codec or canonical encoding is assigned here under (`[ROOT-amend:design:bite-one-internal-envelopes]`).

**Definition (Act carrier)** · `def:acts:carrier` CandidateAct is unchecked input: structural identifier, body, candidate legs, act-owned projections, immutable protocol references, public authentication evidence, finalization epoch and authoritative position. ActId alone contains author and family. ValidatedAct is a distinct owned view with ValidatedLeg values that have no candidate removal slot; dependency_status takes ValidatedRecords and derives an edge's status from its owning act, without storing a second status on the leg. ValidatedRecords exposes borrowed views of its private immutable snapshot, never candidates. Nonempty authentication evidence is retained without certifying authenticity.

**Definition (Local validation)** · `def:acts:validation` validate_record checks a complete candidate batch against ValidatedHistory and opaque declarations, then owns a distinct ValidatedRecords snapshot. Duplicate identities, missing references, missing co-legs, invalid local fields and dependencies return typed ValidationError variants. Validation preserves complete acts, quantities, units, vintages and authoritative positions without an encoding. The unpublished error surface remains exhaustive; InvalidCause, DependencyCause and DeclarationCause distinguish local reasons. Every public operation is counted and timed at every level with an explicit refusal outcome for contract rejection; only trace captures full content. The executable owns bootstrap. There are no optional features or production codec/graph dependencies.


**Definition (Opaque declarations)** · `def:acts:declarations` DeclarationId contains namespace, name and edition; Declaration retains announcement and effective boundaries without a payload. Duplicate identities and simultaneous successors refuse. Empty namespaces, empty names and late announcements have distinct DeclarationCause values; malformed selection queries refuse as InvalidSelectionKey, independently of edition availability. select_effective_declaration selects by effective boundary rather than edition number or input order, retaining historical answers under (`[ROOT-amend:design:bite-one-declaration-selection]`). Bite 2 confirms or explicitly changes the late-announcement rule; declaration contents remain there.

**Definition (Dependency and parent content)** · `def:acts:dependencies` DependencyProjection has FullEmpty, Full and Tombstoned states. Full contains a nonempty, sorted, duplicate-free, self-free list of act identifiers for placement. StructuralBody.asserted_parents is a separate non-removable, sorted, duplicate-free, self-free Vec of ActId; only it defines the causal-parent relation. Both resolve to strictly earlier acts in history or proposals, never leg identifiers. Their derived order is author bytes, then sequence, then family bytes. MAX_DEPENDENCIES and MAX_ASSERTED_PARENTS are provisional 1024-entry bounds; published count parameters and absent identifier byte bounds remain held for bite 2 under (`[ROOT-amend:design:provisional-carriage-bounds]`). This separates the paper’s plain-mentioned ``def:graph:act-causal-parents`` and ``def:graph:act-dependency-projection``; no paper data is transcribed here.

**Definition (Validated history)** · `def:acts:history` validate_history checks previously accepted whole acts and external names before deriving all act and leg references together. It does not certify host admission or cryptographic evidence. validate_record resolves both relation lists against that fixed history and the proposed batch, with strictly earlier target positions. Earlier proposals remain outside history: processing order cannot enlarge it. The owned snapshot admits no isolated history edge or post-validation mutation.

**Definition (Boundary inputs)** · `def:acts:boundary-inputs` assemble_boundary_inputs takes ValidatedRecords and a position cut: history precedes it and proposals are at or after it. It memoizes each proposal’s asserted-parent ancestors outside history once, preserving overlaps and all co-legs inside whole acts. History parents add nothing and tombstoned dependency carriage has no effect. Every closure includes its root; their union is exactly the proposals. Output follows authoritative position with structural identity breaking presentation ties. Work is an optional exact count of node visits, parent inspections, inherited-member insert attempts and emitted members; overflow is unavailable, never zero. Records work_operations adds the same steps to invocation accounting. No partition blocks or exhaustion budget are invented under (`[ROOT-amend:design:boundary-snapshot-representation]`).
