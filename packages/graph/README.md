# Whole-act partition and binary formation · `guide:membership:graph`

**Section (Membership)** · `sec:membership:area-membership` Dropping or duplicating an act across dependent blocks changes the proposed whole-act union and can separate a causal ancestor from its dependent. This first increment implements the records-level partition of (`[ROOT-alg:design:dependency-partition]`), using the closure inputs of (`[ROOT-amend:design:bite-one-closure-inputs]`). It does not turn records validation into family formation or authentication.

**Definition (Dependency partition)** · `def:membership:partition` partition_dependencies takes an immutable BoundaryInputs, whose proposed whole acts, principal closures and fixed pre-epoch history were checked by pn-records. It returns petgraph UnGraph directly, with structural root identities as vertex weights and one least shared act identity per intersection edge, alongside blocks of whole act identities. Vertices follow authoritative root order; each intersecting pair appears once in lexicographic position order. Connected-component unions must cover the proposed identities exactly once before publication. Block members follow authoritative order and blocks follow their first member; blocks may interleave. Shared parents already in history do not join blocks, and removable declared dependencies do not define them. This realizes network-v0.29.2, ``def:graph:dependent-act-set``, within the internal carrier scope. Missing parents, late parents and incomplete paired acts refuse through records before partition. Backend index exhaustion refuses as Capacity, and an inconsistent union refuses as Coverage; allocation failure and an explicit computation budget are not established by these checks.
